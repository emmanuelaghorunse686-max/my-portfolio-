Key Notes (Properties)

  • Closure: If a and b are in set S, then a * b must also be in S.
  • Commutative: a * b = b * a.
  • Associative: (a * b) * c = a * (b * c).
  • Identity (e): a * e = e * a = a.
  • Inverse: a * a⁻¹ = a⁻¹ * a = e.
