Quadratic Equations

Master factorisation, discriminant, and sum/product of roots.

1. Factorisation Method

Key Notes

  • Standard form: \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\)
  • Find two numbers that multiply to \(ac\) and add to \(b\).
  • Split the middle term and factor by grouping.
Q1. Solve \(x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0\)
Find factors of 6 that add to -5: (-2 and -3).
\(x^2 - 2x - 3x + 6 = 0\)
\(x(x - 2) - 3(x - 2) = 0\)
\((x - 2)(x - 3) = 0\)
\(x = 2\) or \(x = 3\)
2. Types of Roots (Discriminant)

Key Notes

  • Discriminant \(D = b^2 - 4ac\).
  • \(D > 0\): Two distinct real roots.
  • \(D = 0\): Two equal real roots.
  • \(D < 0\): No real roots (complex).
Q2. For what values of \(k\) will \(y^2 - (k-2)y + 1 = -2k\) have real equal roots?
A. 0, 12 B. 3, 4 C. 0, 3 D. -3, 4
Rearrange: \(y^2 - (k-2)y + (1+2k) = 0\)
For equal roots, \(b^2 - 4ac = 0\).
\((-k+2)^2 - 4(1)(1+2k) = 0\)
\(k^2 - 4k + 4 - 4 - 8k = 0\)
\(k^2 - 12k = 0\)
\(k(k - 12) = 0\)
Answer: A (k = 0 or 12)
Q3. For what values of \(k\) will \(x^2 - (k+2)x + (2k+1) = 0\) have real equal roots?
A. 0, 4 B. 1, 5 C. -1, 5 D. 5, 7
\(a = 1\), \(b = -(k+2)\), \(c = 2k+1\)
\(b^2 - 4ac = 0\)
\((k+2)^2 - 4(2k+1) = 0\)
\(k^2 + 4k + 4 - 8k - 4 = 0\)
\(k^2 - 4k = 0\)
\(k(k - 4) = 0\)
Answer: A (k = 0 or 4)
3. Sum & Product of Roots (Alpha Beta)

Key Notes

  • Sum of roots: \(\alpha + \beta = -b/a\)
  • Product of roots: \(\alpha\beta = c/a\)
  • Forming equation: \(x^2 - (\text{sum})x + (\text{product}) = 0\)
Q4. If \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are roots of \(2x^2 - 5x + 3 = 0\), find \(\alpha + \beta\).
\(a = 2\), \(b = -5\)
\(\alpha + \beta = -b/a = -(-5)/2\)
Answer: 5/2
Q5. Find the product of the roots of \(3x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0\).
\(a = 3\), \(c = 1\)
\(\alpha\beta = c/a = 1/3\)
Answer: 1/3
4. More Practice (Forming Equations)

Key Notes

  • To form an equation from roots \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\): \(x^2 - (\alpha+\beta)x + \alpha\beta = 0\).
Q6. Form a quadratic equation whose roots are 2 and -3.
Sum = \(2 + (-3) = -1\)
Product = \(2 \times (-3) = -6\)
Equation: \(x^2 - (\text{sum})x + (\text{product}) = 0\)
\(x^2 - (-1)x + (-6) = 0\)
Answer: \(x^2 + x - 6 = 0\)
